# Third-party notices

## Turkish BIN list

`backend/src/services/turkey-bin-data.ts` is generated from
[berkaybucan/turkey-bin-list](https://github.com/berkaybucan/turkey-bin-list)
and is used only to show the issuing bank for the first six digits of a card.
The dataset is not used to approve payments or to store card details.
